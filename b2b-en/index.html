Wake-up · Why now
05

Why is ‘right now’ the most urgent moment?

You still earn

While employed you have the cash flow to act. After retirement, options shrink fast.

Time still compounds

Pension and savings grow with years. Every year you wait, the same result costs more.

Decisions are reversible

Severance payout, early pension claim — once made they can’t be undone. Now you can still choose.

The overlap of urgency and capacity to act is your last golden window.

The moment urgency and capacity overlap is the final golden time to prepare.

Money · The 5-layer pension
10

Retirement income has five layers — base + top-up

Layer 1
National Pension
Lifelong, inflation-linked — the floor of your income
Layer 2
Retirement Pension
Bridges the gap between exit and pension start
Layer 3
Private Pension
The biggest variable you control — fills the shortfall
Layer 4
Housing Pension
Turns your home into lifelong monthly cash
Layer 5
Work / other income
A second stage of earning after your main career
On top of the layers the system gives you, the layer you fill yourself makes up half of retirement.
